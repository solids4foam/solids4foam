# Cardiac elastodynamics benchmark, idealised left ventricle: `ArosticaEtAl2025/benchmark1`

## Tutorial Aims

- Reproduce benchmark 1 (monoventricular mechanics) of Aróstica et al. [1]:
  steps 0A, 0B, 1 and the blinded step 2 cases A–C.
- Demonstrate the `viscoActiveHolzapfelOgden` law: an orthotropic
  Holzapfel–Ogden myocardium with a fibre-compression switch, a viscous term
  and a time-dependent active stress.
- Demonstrate the `solidSpringDashpot` boundary condition (pericardium and
  base Robin conditions) and the `setEllipsoidFibreField` utility.

```note
Like LandEtAl2015/problem3, this case needs solids4foam built with PETSc and
an OpenFOAM.com version; otherwise Allrun exits without running.
```

## Case Overview

**Geometry** (eq. 9–11 of [1]). A truncated ellipsoid with its long axis
along x:

```text
x = ( r_long cos μ, r_short sin μ cos θ, r_short sin μ sin θ )
```

with endocardium r_short = 25 mm, r_long = 90 mm, μ ∈ [−π, −acos(5/17)] and
epicardium r_short = 35 mm, r_long = 97 mm, μ ∈ [−π, −acos(5/20)]. The base
joins the two rims with a straight line in the meridian, so Γ_top is a shallow
cone (x = 26.5 mm on the endocardium, 24.25 mm on the epicardium), exactly as
in the reference gmsh script of the benchmark toolkit.

**Mesh.** Two mesh types, chosen with `MESH_TYPE`:

- `ogrid` (default): an all-hexahedral mesh with an O-grid at the apex,
  written directly as `constant/polyMesh` by `scripts/makeOGridMesh.py`
  (levels 1–2 are shipped in `verification/mesh/ogrid`; levels 3–4
  are generated by `Allrun`, which needs `python3` with `numpy`). Endo- and
  epicardium are exact ellipsoids; nodes through the wall interpolate
  linearly between them.
- `revolved`: as in LandEtAl2015/problem3, `blockMesh` makes a
  one-cell-thick slice of the wall, `extrudeMesh` revolves it through 360°
  about x and `createPatch` removes the empty patches. This puts a fan of
  wedge cells on the axis at the apex. In the first runs these cells lost
  volume (J ≈ 0.84 by t = 0.4 s), and the run failed with a negative J
  near the apex at t = 0.516 s, so it is no longer the default.

Patches: `endo`, `epi`, `base`.

| `MESH_NUMBER` | O-grid: square side × ring × wall | revolved | cells |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 × 18 × 3 | 20 × 3 × 32 | 1 920 |
| 2 (default) | 16 × 36 × 5 | 40 × 5 × 64 | 12 800 |
| 3 | 32 × 72 × 10 | 80 × 10 × 128 | 102 400 |
| 4 | 64 × 144 × 20 | 160 × 20 × 256 | 819 200 |

**Fibres** (Section 3.2). `setEllipsoidFibreField` solves Δt̄ = 0 with
t̄ = 0 on `endo`, 1 on `epi`, zero gradient on `base`, then builds the
ellipsoidal basis (e_μ, e_θ) with radii interpolated in t̄ and writes

```text
f0 = sin α e_μ + cos α e_θ,  n0 = e_μ × e_θ,  s0 = f0 × n0,
α(t̄) = −60° + 120° t̄.
```

It is a direct port of the reference implementation's `compute_system()`.

**Material** (Section 2.2, Tables 1–2; `constant/mechanicalProperties`):

```text
S = ∂Ψ_aniso/∂E + η Ė + τ(t) f0⊗f0
Ψ_aniso = a/2b (e^{b(Ī1−3)} − 1)
        + Σ_{i=f,s} a_i/2b_i χ(I4i)(e^{b_i(I4i−1)²} − 1)
        + a_fs/2b_fs (e^{b_fs I8fs²} − 1) + κ/4 (J² − 1 − 2 ln J)
```

with χ(x) = 1/(1 + e^{−k(x−1)}), k = 100 (its derivative is included, as in
the reference codes, which differentiate Ψ automatically), κ = 10⁶ Pa,
η = 100 Pa s, ρ = 1000 kg/m³. Ė is the change of E over the time step.

**Active stress and pressure** (eq. 5–8, Tables 3–4). Both are ODEs in time
only, so they are solved once by `scripts/makeTimeSeries.py` (an exact
exponential integrator, h = 10 µs) and tabulated in `constant/timeSeries`.
The peaks are τ_max = 118 106 Pa and p_max = 16 074 Pa. The paper quotes
118 817 and 16 118 Pa; a tightly toleranced Radau solve gives the values
used here, so the paper's figures carry the error of a loose ODE tolerance.

**Boundary conditions** (eq. 1, Table 1; `0.orig/D`):

| Patch | Condition | Parameters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `endo` | `solidTraction`, follower pressure | `constant/timeVsPressure` |
| `epi` | `solidSpringDashpot`, normal only | α = 1e8 Pa/m, β = 5e3 Pa s/m |
| `base` | `solidSpringDashpot`, all directions | α = 1e5 Pa/m, β = 5e3 Pa s/m |

**Formulation.** `./Allrun ... pressure` (default) uses the mixed
displacement-pressure formulation (`solvePressure true`): the law hands its
κ-penalty term dU/dJ back separately and the solver replaces it by a pressure
that obeys the same penalty relation, so the material is unchanged.
`./Allrun ... displacement` keeps κ in the momentum equation. In the
displacement formulation the implicit stiffness that scales the FV
stabilisation is ≈ κ = 10⁶ Pa, four orders above the matrix shear modulus
(a = 59 Pa), which risks an artificially stiff response; LandEtAl2015/problem3
shows a 36 % difference between the two formulations for the same reason.

**Solver.** Total-Lagrangian, cell-centred FV
(`nonLinearGeometryTotalLagrangianTotalDisplacement`), PETSc SNES Newton with
a matrix-free Jacobian and a backtracking line search, BDF2 (`backward`) in
time, Δt = 1 ms, t ∈ [0, 1] s.
The displacement at p0 = (0.025, 0.03, 0) m and p1 = (0, 0.03, 0) m is
written every step by two `solidPointDisplacement` function objects.

## New code used by this case

- `viscoActiveHolzapfelOgden`
  (`src/solids4FoamModels/mechanicalConstitutiveLaw/mechanicalConstitutiveLaws`):
  the material law.
- `solidSpringDashpot` (`src/solids4FoamModels/solidModels/fvPatchFields`):
  the spring-dashpot (Robin) condition, nominal traction
  −(K u + C u̇) with separate normal and tangential coefficients. Here
  `kNormal = kTangential = α` and `cNormal = cTangential = β` on `base`,
  and `kTangential = cTangential = 0` on `epi` (normal only).
- `setEllipsoidFibreField` (`applications/utilities`): t̄, f0, s0 and n0 for
  the ellipsoid.

Rebuild after pulling them in: `./Allwmake` at the top of the repository.

## Running the Case

```bash
./Allrun                          # step 1, mesh 2
./Allrun step0A                   # any step
MESH_NUMBER=3 ./Allrun step2B          # pressure formulation: serial only
./Allrun step1 displacement parallel  # displacement formulation runs in parallel
MESH_TYPE=revolved ./Allrun step1   # the LandEtAl2015-style mesh
python3 scripts/checkJacobian.py    # min/max J = det(F) at each write
./Allclean
```

If `python3` with `numpy` (and optionally `matplotlib`) is available, `Allrun`
finishes with `scripts/compareWithReference.py <step>`, which prints the
largest difference from the median of the participants' solutions and writes
`verification/<step>.png` in the style of Fig. 8–10 of [1].
The reference curves in `verification/reference/<step>/` are the submitted
solutions distributed with the cardiac-benchmark-toolkit (MIT licence) [2].

## Expected Results

The participants' solutions in [1] agree to within about 0.5 mm. For step 1
the x-displacement of p0 reaches about −35 mm at t ≈ 0.48 s and that of p1
about −27 mm (Fig. 10 of [1]).

Results so far (O-grid mesh 2, pressure formulation, serial; every step
converges in 3–4 Newton iterations):

- **step0A** (active only): the timing of contraction and relaxation follows
  the participants'. Peak long-axis displacements are within about 2 mm
  (p0 x −31.8 vs ≈ −30 mm, p1 x −23.5 vs ≈ −22 mm); the radial displacement
  of p1 is too large (−3.5 vs ≈ −2.6 mm).
- **step1** (full beat): filling, onset and relaxation follow the
  participants'; during systole the long-axis shortening is 3–5 mm too large
  (p0 x −38.4 vs −35.4 mm at t = 0.45 s) and the twist of p1 is about 1 mm
  too small. The twist about the long axis is only held by the soft base
  springs, and in step 1 it is the small difference between the large passive
  rotation (≈ 16 mm in step 0B) and the active counter-rotation, so it is the
  most sensitive output.

The results depend on the FV stabilisation, which is scaled by the
`tangentShearModulus` of the law (default a + (af + as + afs)/6); including
the viscous η/Δt in it, as an earlier version did, made the response
markedly too stiff. Mesh convergence (`MESH_NUMBER=3`) is still to be run.

```note
Other differences to expect from the discretisation: a cell-centred
finite-volume method instead of P1/P2 finite elements, BDF2 instead of
generalised-α time stepping (which damps the oscillations after t = 0.55 s
differently), and a first-order Ė in the viscous term.
```

```note
The mixed (pressure) formulation runs in serial only: the pressure-displacement
coupling of its Jacobian is not implemented on processor boundaries
(`foamPetscSnesHelper::InsertFvmDivUIntoPETScMatrix`), and `Allrun` refuses
the combination.
```
